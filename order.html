WHAT'S IN THE BOX +
INGREDIENTS & ALLERGENS +

Ingredients vary by flavour. Please contact Gookie if you need the ingredient information for a specific cookie before ordering.

Our cookies contain or may contain:

  • Wheat (Gluten)
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Soy
  • Peanuts
  • Tree Nuts, including Macadamia and Pistachio

All Gookies are prepared in the same kitchen and may come into contact with other allergens. We cannot guarantee that any product is completely free from allergen cross-contact.

STORAGE & REHEATING +

Best enjoyed within 3 days. Keep in an airtight container at room temperature, away from direct sunlight and heat.

Freeze in an airtight container or freezer-safe bag for up to 2 months.

Microwave: 8–12 seconds.
Air fryer: 150°C for 2–3 minutes.
