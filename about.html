HOW IT STARTED

One mixer.
One oven.
One serious cookie obsession.

Gookie started small in Ampang, Selangor — with lots of testing, plenty of butter and a very clear goal: create chunky cookies with a crisp edge, a soft chewy centre and enough personality to make people remember them.

What began as a kitchen idea slowly became a full cookie crew, each flavour with its own mood, texture and little bit of Gookie charm.

“You’re sweeter than our cookies.”

Less sweet is part of the Gookie DNA.

WHAT MAKES A GOOKIE?
