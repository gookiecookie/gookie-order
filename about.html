HOW IT STARTED

We just wanted a really good cookie.

Gookie started with one simple obsession: making the kind of soft cookie we actually wanted to eat — chunky, satisfying and not overwhelmingly sweet.

So we kept mixing, baking, tweaking and eating an unreasonable number of test cookies. Eventually, Gookie happened.
