KEEP THE GOOD STUFF GOOD

Storage &
reheating.

HOW SHOULD I STORE MY GOOKIES?+

Keep your cookies sealed in an airtight container and away from heat and direct sunlight. For longer storage, you can freeze them.

CAN I FREEZE THEM?+

Yes. Wrap or seal the cookies well before freezing to protect their texture and flavour.

HOW DO I REHEAT A GOOKIE?+

Warm it gently until the centre is soft and the chocolate starts to melt. Heating time varies by appliance, so start short and add a little more time if needed.

DO I HAVE TO REHEAT THEM?+

Nope. Gookies can be enjoyed at room temperature too. Reheating is simply for that fresh-from-the-oven feeling.
